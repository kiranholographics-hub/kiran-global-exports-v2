# Article backlog

Topics queued for future drafts, one per "day" of writing. Titles only
here — each becomes a full draft (same header format as the numbered
files) before it is ready to publish.

Every topic on this list is chosen the same way the five already
written were: something a real buyer would search for or ask before
naming a supplier, answerable honestly from what is already true about
this business, and ending where an inquiry starts. None of them is a
city or a country — those live in `seoLandingPages.js`, not here, and
only get added where the business genuinely wants that market (see the
note in SEO-ROUTINE.md on doorway pages).

## Publishing note

Writing one a day and publishing one a day are different things. The
database only accepts connections once Network Access is opened (see
SEO-ROUTINE.md), so publishing happens whenever that is open — daily if
you want to open it daily, or in a batch every week or two. Either way
Google records the real date the article actually went live; there is
nothing to fake and nothing gained by trying to.
