# Outreach list: getting Kiran Global Exports onto existing lists

For Riya and Raj sir. Built 13 Nov 2026 from live web searches. Nothing
has been sent yet.

This is the part of the strategy in Raj sir's video that is safe: find
articles and lists that name other towel suppliers but not us, and ask
the author to add us. It needs no paid tool. The third idea in the video
(automatic link swaps between businesses) is **not** used: Google's spam
policy forbids link exchanges done for ranking, and the site could be
penalised.

## Rules

1. **Never pay for a link or a listing.** Several "top 5" articles below
   are sponsored posts and may reply with a price. Say thank you and stop.
   Paid links without a "sponsored" tag break Google's rules.
2. **Never offer a link back in exchange.** No swaps.
3. **One email, one polite follow-up after 7 days, then stop.**
4. **Only true facts** (Part 1 of `link-kit.md`). No client names, no
   prices, no unconfirmed certifications.
5. **Find the right address on the site itself:** the article's author,
   an "Editorial", "Contact" or "Write for us" page. If there is only a
   contact form, paste the email text into the form.
6. **Look for dead entries (strategy 2).** Before writing, click every
   company link in the article. If one is broken, or the company has
   closed, mention it in the email (Template B). Editors fix broken
   links, and it is the best reason for them to add us.
7. Fill in the tracker (Part 4) after every email.
