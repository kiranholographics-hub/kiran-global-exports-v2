# Off-site link kit: Kiran Global Exports

For Riya. Everything here is ready to copy and paste. Nothing has been
submitted anywhere yet.

Why this matters: Google ranks a new website higher when other real
websites mention it and link to it. The website already has enough
pages. What it does not have yet is other sites pointing to it. Each
listing below is one more of those.

Rules for every listing:

- Copy the name, address and phone **exactly** as in Part 1. The same
  spelling and the same spaces, everywhere. Google matches listings to the
  business by these three lines.
- Use only real photos: products on the website, the office, the team (with
  their consent). No stock photos, no AI images.
- No prices, no MOQ, no delivery times. We quote per specification.
- Do not buy links, reviews or "SEO packages". They can get the website
  penalised.
- After each listing, fill in the tracker (Part 9).
